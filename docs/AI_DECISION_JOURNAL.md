# AI Decision Journal

This is a lightweight record for durable architecture decisions made during mentored work.

Do not record minor coding choices. Use this only when a decision affects responsibility, invariants, domain behavior,
testing strategy, or future design direction.

## Entry Template

```md
## YYYY-MM-DD - <decision>

Context:
- <issue or feature>

Decision:
- <chosen responsibility, boundary, invariant, or rule>

Alternatives considered:
- <alternative and why it was rejected>

Evidence:
- <repository evidence, external evidence, user-confirmed domain behavior, or inference>

Verification:
- <tests, manual checks, or reasoning that protect the decision>
```
